# Product and search-intent research

Research date: 26 November 2026. Sources are the competitors' own public product pages. This is a feature and information-architecture review, not an estimate of their traffic, rankings, authority, conversion rate or organic keyword difficulty. Page claims are attributed to their owners; no files or private inputs were submitted. Mobile rendering, result quality and accessibility were not independently benchmarked for these competitors.

## Product decisions supported by this research

1. Favor a usable input-first workflow, clear state, a measurable output and the relevant next task over long introductory copy.
2. Keep synonyms on one canonical operation: photo/image/picture compression; merge/combine PDF; PNG-to-JPG/JPEG. Pixel dimensions and file-byte targets are distinct controls.
3. Preserve local processing as an accurate, configuration-driven property, including server and third-party exceptions. Do not copy unverified retention, security or quality guarantees.
4. Add target presets only when keyword data and actual functionality support them. An exact target needs an honest “could not reach target” result; a resized photo must preserve the user's chosen crop/aspect behavior.
5. Treat useful examples, formulas, limits, scan tests and output previews as product improvements. The research does not establish that copying a competitor's wording, design or number of pages improves rankings.

## Remaining evidence gaps

Authenticated plans, paid provider agreements, competitors' actual file outputs, real-device scan reliability and mobile performance were not tested. Exact allowances and retention policies can change and must be rechecked before any comparative public claim. Organic competition evidence still requires sampled query results and/or a legitimate SEO data source; advertising competition from Keyword Planner is not a substitute.
